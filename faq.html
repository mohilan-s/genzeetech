Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding our digital engineering, cyber security practices, and Academy streams.

We are proudly headquartered in Colombo, Sri Lanka, and collaborate seamlessly with clients across the UK, Australia, UAE, Singapore, US, and beyond through modern remote collaboration tools.
We provide Website Development (WordPress/React), Full-Stack Systems (Next.js/Node.js), Mobile Apps (React Native), Branding & UI/UX Design, Cyber Security Solutions (audits & pen testing), and GenZee Tech Academy for school students.
We begin with an initial scope assessment of your web systems or servers. We run non-destructive vulnerability scans, identify security loopholes, and present an executive summary alongside technical remediation steps.
Our Academy welcomes school students from Grade 1 through Grade 11. We offer tailored streams: Junior Explorers (Grade 1-5), Digital Creators (Grade 6-8), and Future Innovators (Grade 9-11 / O/L ICT).
For online batches, a computer/laptop with internet access and webcam is required. For in-person sessions at our Colombo center, workstations are provided, though students may bring their own laptops.
A standard responsive business website typically takes 2 to 4 weeks, while complex full-stack web applications and mobile apps usually range from 6 to 12 weeks depending on feature scope.
