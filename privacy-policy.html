Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: February 2026 • Colombo, Sri Lanka

At GenZee Tech (Pvt) Ltd (“we”, “our”, or “us”), we take your privacy and the confidentiality of your personal and enterprise data seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect information across our corporate digital solutions and our educational wing, GenZee Tech Academy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal and business information strictly when you voluntarily submit it via our contact forms, registration portals, or direct communication:

  • Client Project Inquiries: Full name, corporate email address, WhatsApp/phone number, organization name, and technical project requirements.
  • Academy Enrollments: Parent/guardian full name, contact phone number, email address, student first name, and current school grade.
  • Technical Analytics: Anonymized server telemetry, browser user-agents, and IP addresses to maintain security and prevent automated malicious attacks.

2. Special Protection for Children's Data (GenZee Tech Academy)

We adhere strictly to international child data protection principles and COPPA best practices:

  • Enrollment forms must be completed exclusively by an adult parent or legal guardian.
  • We collect only the bare minimum student information required for academic batch placement (first name and grade level).
  • We never publish student photos, surnames, contact details, or home addresses anywhere without explicit prior written parental consent.
  • Student project showcases in our academy gallery display student first names or creative coding aliases only.

3. How We Use Your Data

Collected information is utilized strictly to:

  • Respond promptly to project inquiries, cybersecurity audit bookings, and quotation requests.
  • Coordinate class schedules, batch confirmations, and student progress reports for enrolled parents.
  • Fulfill contracted software development deliverables and offer post-launch technical maintenance.

4. Data Security & Non-Disclosure

We employ enterprise-grade security standards, including TLS 1.3 encrypted data transmission, restricted-access encrypted databases, and strict access controls. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or business data to third-party advertisers or brokers under any circumstances.
